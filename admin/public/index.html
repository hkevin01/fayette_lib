⚡ Power Outage Recovery

In case of power outage or server crash:

  1. Services are configured with restart: unless-stopped in Docker
  2. They will automatically restart when power is restored
  3. Your session will be lost (you'll need to log in again)
  4. All data is persisted in volumes — no data loss
  5. Check System Health above to confirm all services are running

💡 For critical infrastructure, consider adding a backup power supply (UPS) or failover server.
