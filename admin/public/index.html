⚡ Power Outage Recovery

In case of power outage or server crash:

  1. Services are configured with restart: unless-stopped in Docker
  2. They will automatically restart when power is restored
  3. Your session will be lost (you'll need to log in again)
  4. All data is persisted in volumes — no data loss
  5. Check System Health above to confirm all services are running

💡 For critical infrastructure, consider adding a backup power supply (UPS) or failover server.

🧾 Staff Renewal Checklist

  1. 60 days before due date: confirm domain + hosting expiration dates and verify account login works.
  2. 45 days before due date: confirm budget line and payment method with administration.
  3. 30 days before due date: renew domain and hosting terms in provider portal (recommend 1-year minimum).
  4. Immediately after payment: download invoice/receipt and save to shared finance records.
  5. Same day: update this Hosting tab with new renewal date, receipt location, and any account changes.
  6. If blocked: escalate to IT contact and Library Director before due date to prevent service interruption.

Tip: Use a shared calendar reminder at 60/45/30/14 days before expiration.
