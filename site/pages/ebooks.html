🚀 How to Get Started
  1. Get your library card — Visit any FCPL branch with proof of Fayette County residency. Cards are free!
  2. Download the app — Get the Libby app from your device's app store (iOS or Android), or access Hoopla on the web.
  3. Sign in with your library card — Search for "Fayette County Public Libraries" and enter your card number and PIN.
  4. Browse and borrow — Find a title you like and check it out instantly!

Need help setting up digital access? Visit any branch and staff will walk you through it — or call 304-465-0121.
