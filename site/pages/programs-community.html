🏘️ Community Programs

FCPL serves all of Fayette County — our library is a community hub for everyone.

📅 Community Events & Programs

FCPL hosts and participates in community events throughout the year. Here are some of the programs and services we offer to the wider Fayette County community:

  • Annual Book Sale — gently used books at bargain prices, usually held in the spring and fall
  • Holiday Events & Special Programs — seasonal celebrations and themed programs throughout the year
  • Community Meeting Rooms — available for local groups and organizations at no charge
  • Voter Registration Assistance — staff can help you register to vote at all branches
  • Genealogy Research Assistance — staff help with family history research, access to Ancestry and other databases
  • Notary Services — available at select branches (call ahead to confirm)
  • Job & Resume Help — staff can assist with computer access, printing, and online job applications

🏢 Community Meeting Rooms

FCPL branches offer meeting room space to community groups, nonprofits, and local organizations — free of charge, subject to availability.

  • Available during library hours
  • Suitable for meetings, study groups, and community gatherings
  • Wi-Fi available
  • Advance reservation required

To reserve a meeting room, call 304-465-0121 or visit your local branch.

📢 Have a Community Announcement?

📢
Have a community event you'd like to announce to Fayette County? Contact us at 304-465-0121 to discuss posting on our announcements board or community bulletin board.

We're happy to help get the word out about free community events, nonprofit programs, and public services.
