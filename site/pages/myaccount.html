🔑 PIN & Account Help

Forgot Your PIN?

Your PIN is set when you register for a card. If you've forgotten it, we can reset it for you quickly.

  • Call any branch and a librarian will reset it for you
  • Visit in person with your photo ID
  • Default PIN for new cards is the last 4 digits of your phone number

Lost or Expired Card?

Cards are free to replace and must be renewed every 2 years.

  • Bring photo ID to any branch for a replacement
  • Cards expire every 2 years — renewal is free and instant
  • Your borrowing history and holds are preserved

🪪 Don't Have a Card Yet? It's Free!

A library card is completely free for all Fayette County residents and opens the door to all of FCPL's services — including eBooks, digital streaming, research databases, and more.

✅ What to bring:

  • Photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
  • Proof of Fayette County address (utility bill, lease, or mail)
  • Children under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign

🎁 What your card gets you:

  • Borrow up to 10 items at a time
  • Free eBooks & audiobooks via Libby
  • Free streaming via Hoopla Digital
  • Computer and internet access at all branches
  • Interlibrary loans from any library in WV
  • Access to research databases
