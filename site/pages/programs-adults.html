📚 Adult Book Club

📚

FCPL Adult Book Club

Location: Oak Hill Branch, 531 Summit St, Oak Hill, WV

Schedule: Monthly — check the calendar below for exact dates.

Love to read? Join your neighbors for monthly discussions of great books. All genres welcome. New members always welcome — no commitment required!

To find out the current title and next meeting date, call 304-465-0121 or visit the Oak Hill Branch. Copies of the current selection are available at the library.

👩‍🍳 Library Chef

👩‍🍳

Library Chef — Free Recipes & Meal Planning

Library Chef gives you access to thousands of professionally developed recipes by top chefs. Build a personal digital cookbook, create weekly meal plans, and generate smart shopping lists.

All free with your FCPL library card.

  • Access to thousands of curated recipes from professional chefs
  • Build and save your own personal recipe collection
  • Create weekly meal plans with drag-and-drop ease
  • Auto-generate shopping lists from any recipe or meal plan
  • Works on phone, tablet, or computer
