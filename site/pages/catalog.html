🪪 Get a Library Card — It's Free!

You need a library card to check out materials and access digital resources. Getting a card is completely free for all Fayette County residents!

✅ What you need to bring:

  • Photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
  • Proof of Fayette County address (utility bill, lease, or mail)
  • Children under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign

🎁 What your card gets you:

  • Borrow up to 10 books at a time
  • Free eBooks and audiobooks via Libby
  • Free streaming via Hoopla Digital
  • Access to research databases
  • Computer and internet access at all branches
  • Interlibrary loans from any library in WV

💡 Search Tips

📖 Finding Books

  • Search by the exact title for best results
  • Use the author's last name to find all their books
  • Try a subject search to browse by topic (e.g., "Gardening" or "Civil War")
  • Use the ISBN number (on the back of a book) for exact matches

📦 Placing a Hold

  • If a book is checked out, you can place a hold — you'll be notified when it's ready
  • Holds are free and you pick up at your preferred branch
  • You need to be signed in to your account to place holds
  • Need help? Ask a librarian — we're glad to assist!
