About Fayette County Public Libraries

Serving the communities of Fayette County, West Virginia since 1935.

🌟 Our Mission

The mission of Fayette County Public Libraries is to provide free access to information, education, culture, and recreation for all residents of Fayette County. We are committed to intellectual freedom, lifelong learning, and serving as a welcoming community space for everyone.

✅ Free Services
  • Book, magazine, and audiobook circulation
  • eBooks & digital media via Libby / Hoopla
  • Computer and internet access
  • Fax service (local & long distance)
  • Notary Public
  • Voter registration assistance
  • Children's programs & storytimes
  • Adult programming and book clubs
  • Research and homework help
  • Bookmobile home delivery for qualifying patrons
  • Homebound delivery service
  • Genealogy research assistance
  • Community meeting room use (by reservation)
💲 Fee-Based Services
  • Photocopies — $0.15 / page (black & white)
  • Color copies — $0.25 / page
  • Printing from computer — $0.15 / page
  • Laminating service
  • Lost card replacement — $1.00
  • Overdue fines (per library policy)

📋 Patron Policy Summary

Library Card Eligibility

Any resident of Fayette County, WV is eligible for a free library card. Proof of county residency is required. Non-residents may obtain a card for an annual fee.

Overdue Fines

Fines accrue for overdue materials. Lost or damaged items are charged at replacement cost plus a processing fee. Accounts with balances over $10.00 are suspended until resolved.

Computer Use Policy

Computers are available for patron use during library hours. Sessions are typically 60 minutes per patron. Use of library computers for illegal activities is prohibited and will result in loss of privileges and potential legal action.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a library card?

Visit any FCPL branch with proof of Fayette County residency (driver's license, utility bill, etc.). Cards are free for county residents.

Can I use computers at the library?

Yes! Free internet access is available at all branches. Printing is available at $0.15/page (B&W) and $0.25/page (color).

How do I access eBooks and digital media?

Download the free Libby app (OverDrive) or visit Hoopla online. You'll need your FCPL library card number to sign in.

What if I can't come to the library?

We offer a Homebound Delivery Program for patrons who are unable to visit due to health or mobility issues. Learn more →

Does the Bookmobile come to my area?

The FCPL Bookmobile serves schools and communities throughout the county. Check the schedule →

How do I renew items?

Renew online via the WV Info Depot catalog, by phone, or in person at any branch.

Can I request items from other libraries?

Yes! Through interlibrary loan, we can request materials from other WV libraries and beyond. Ask a librarian for assistance.

🏛️ Board of Directors

The Fayette County Public Library Board of Directors provides governance and oversight for the library system. The Board meets regularly to set policy, approve budgets, and ensure the library serves all community members effectively.

📅
Board meetings are open to the public. Contact the library at 304-465-0121 for the current meeting schedule and agenda.
