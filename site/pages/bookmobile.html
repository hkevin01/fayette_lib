📚 Bookmobile

The library comes to you — serving schools and communities throughout Fayette County.

🚌 About the Bookmobile

The Fayette County Public Library Bookmobile brings library services directly to you! Our bookmobile visits schools, community centers, and rural areas throughout the county that may not have easy access to a branch location.

Services Available on the Bookmobile

  • Full library checkout — books, audiobooks, DVDs
  • New library card sign-up
  • Hold pickup for items requested at branches
  • Children's programming at school stops
  • Summer reading program participation
  • Material returns and renewals
