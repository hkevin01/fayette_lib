📖 Adult Book Club

📚

FCPL Adult Book Club

Location: Oak Hill Branch, 531 Summit St, Oak Hill, WV

Schedule: Monthly — check the calendar for exact dates.

Love to read? Join your neighbors for monthly discussions of great books. All genres welcome. New members always welcome!

To find out the current title and next meeting date, call 304-465-0121 or visit the Oak Hill Branch. Copies of the current selection are available at the library.

Teen Programs Coming Soon!
FCPL is developing programming specifically for teens. Contact your local branch to express interest or suggest ideas. Your input shapes our programs!

In the meantime, teens are always welcome at all library programs, and can access:

  • Free computer and internet access
  • Homework help from staff
  • TutorWV — free online tutoring (WV state program via Tutor.com)
  • eBooks, graphic novels, and audiobooks via Libby & Hoopla
  • All research databases through Research & Homework
