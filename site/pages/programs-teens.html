🧑 Teens at the Library

FCPL is your space — free resources, internet, homework help, and more for Fayette County teens.

🧑
Teen Programs Coming Soon!
FCPL is actively developing programming specifically for teens. Contact your local branch to express interest or suggest ideas — your input shapes what we build!

✅ Available Right Now

Teens are welcome at all library programs. Plus, the library offers these resources specifically for you:

💻 Free Internet & Computers

Access the internet, print documents, and use library computers at every branch — no cost, no time limit during open hours.

📚 Homework Help

Library staff are here to help. Ask at any branch for assistance finding resources, understanding assignments, or locating books.

🎓 TutorWV — Free Online Tutoring

West Virginia's free online tutoring service through Tutor.com. Get live, one-on-one help with math, science, English, and more — available 24/7 with your WV student ID.

Start Tutoring Session →

📱 eBooks & Graphic Novels

Thousands of eBooks, graphic novels, manga, and audiobooks via Libby and Hoopla — free with your FCPL card.

Browse Now →

🔬 Research Databases

Full access to academic research databases, encyclopedias, and homework help resources — free through FCPL.

Explore Databases →

📖 Library Catalog

Browse FCPL's full collection — books, DVDs, audiobooks, magazines, and graphic novels. Request items from any branch.

Search Catalog →

🪪 Get Your Free Library Card

All Fayette County residents can get a free library card. With a card you can:

  • Borrow books, DVDs, audiobooks, and more
  • Access Libby and Hoopla for unlimited digital content
  • Use all research databases from home or the library
  • Print and use library computers

Sign up at any FCPL branch — just bring a photo ID and proof of Fayette County address. It takes about 5 minutes!

Find Your Nearest Branch →

We want to know what programs you want! We're actively looking for ideas for teen programming — gaming nights, art workshops, coding, creative writing, and more are all on the table.

Call us at 304-465-0121 or stop by any branch to share your ideas.
