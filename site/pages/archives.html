🗂️ Archives & Genealogy

Explore our historic collections — perfect for genealogy research, local history, and West Virginia studies.

📍
Located at the Oak Hill Branch — Most archive materials are housed at the main Oak Hill branch. Please call 304-465-0121 before visiting to confirm availability of specific collections.

👇 Click a tab below to explore each collection

🎞️ Microfilm Collection

Our microfilm collection preserves historical newspapers, government records, and documents from Fayette County and surrounding areas. Microfilm readers are available at the Oak Hill Branch.

📋
What's on microfilm? — Historical newspapers, deeds, census records, and more. Download our full collection guide for details.
📥 Download Collection Guide

File format: Microsoft Word (.docx). You may need Word or a compatible app to open it.

How to Use Microfilm Readers

  1. Ask a librarian to retrieve the microfilm reel you need.
  2. Thread the film through the reader following the labeled guides.
  3. Use the zoom and focus controls to view the image clearly.
  4. To print, use the reader's built-in print function (cost: $0.15/page).
  5. Rewind and return the reel to a librarian when finished.
🎓 School Yearbooks

We collect and preserve Fayette County school yearbooks. Yearbooks are a wonderful resource for family history research — they contain class photos, faculty listings, club memberships, and sports records.

⚠️
Yearbooks are reference materials and may not be checked out. Photocopies available for $0.15/page.

To Find a Yearbook

Contact us at 304-465-0121 to ask about yearbooks for a specific school and year. We'll check availability and have it ready for your visit.

📰 Bound & Loose Newspapers

Our newspaper collection includes both bound volumes and loose copies of local and regional newspapers. These are valuable for genealogy, local history, and research into historical events.

💡
Tip for genealogy researchers: Historic newspapers often contain birth announcements, obituaries, wedding notices, and community news that isn't available in official records.

Online Newspaper Resources

  • Newspapers.com — search historical newspapers nationwide
  • Chronicling America (Library of Congress) — free historical US newspapers
  • WV Encyclopedia — West Virginia history and culture
📖 Magazines

We maintain a collection of older magazine issues, particularly those with local, regional, or West Virginia content. These cover topics from community events to feature articles about Fayette County history.

For current magazine subscriptions, visit any branch — we receive numerous periodicals for patron use in-library.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Collections

Our family collections contain genealogical materials donated by local families — documents, photographs, family trees, and personal histories related to Fayette County residents and families.

✅
Donating family materials? — We welcome donations of genealogical materials, family histories, and photographs related to Fayette County families. Please call or stop by the Oak Hill Branch to discuss.

Genealogy Research Help

Our staff can assist you with genealogy research. For appointments or to inquire about specific family names, call 304-465-0121.

⛰️ The West Virginia Collection

The West Virginia Collection is a curated selection of books, pamphlets, and documents specifically about West Virginia history, culture, geography, and notable persons. This is an essential resource for anyone researching the Mountain State.

📚
Many WV Collection items are reference-only and cannot be checked out. Photocopies are available to researchers.

Online WV History Resources

  • WV Encyclopedia — comprehensive online reference
  • WV State Archives — official state historical records
  • WVU Libraries — West Virginia University collections
📦 Miscellaneous Archives

This section contains additional archival materials that don't fit neatly into other categories — maps, government documents, community records, ephemera, and other unique resources.
