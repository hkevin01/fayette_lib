Bookmobile Driver / Library Assistant

Fayette County Public Libraries — Oak Hill, WV

Now Hiring
📍 Oak Hill, WV (countywide travel)
⏱️ Full-Time
💵 ~$25,000/year
🏛️ State Benefits Package

Position Summary

The Fayette County Public Libraries is seeking a responsible and community-oriented individual to serve as Bookmobile Driver and Library Assistant. This full-time position involves driving our library bookmobile throughout Fayette County to provide library services to schools, underserved communities, and homebound patrons.

Key Responsibilities

  • Safely operate the FCPL bookmobile vehicle on established routes throughout Fayette County
  • Maintain a valid Commercial Driver's License (CDL) or obtain one within required timeframe
  • Perform daily vehicle inspections and maintain vehicle in good working condition
  • Assist library patrons of all ages in selecting and checking out materials
  • Set up and break down bookmobile at stop locations
  • Maintain accurate circulation records and inventory
  • Assist with collection maintenance and delivery of materials to homebound patrons
  • Collaborate with branch staff and Director on scheduling and programming

Qualifications Required

  • High school diploma or GED
  • Valid WV driver's license with clean driving record
  • Ability to obtain CDL (Class B) within 6 months of hire
  • Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and perform physical tasks required for vehicle operations
  • Customer service experience preferred
  • Basic computer skills

Compensation & Benefits

  • Salary: approximately $25,000/year (commensurate with experience)
  • Health insurance
  • Life insurance
  • Retirement plan (PERS)
  • Paid holidays and leave as per WV state policy
