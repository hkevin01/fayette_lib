Programs & Events

Free programs for all ages — children, teens, and adults.

Library Chef — Free Cooking Classes

Build your personal digital cookbook with access to thousands of recipes from top chefs. Library Chef is available FREE to all FCPL cardholders.

Learn anytime, anywhere — no Zoom required! Interactive cooking classes on demand.

Adult Book Club

The FCPL Adult Book Club meets on the 2nd Tuesday of each month at 1:00 PM at the Administration Building on Summit Street, Oak Hill.

All adults are welcome! Call 304-465-0121 for the current book selection.

Summer Reading Program

Every summer, FCPL hosts a reading program for children and teens. Prizes and special events throughout the summer.

Call any branch or watch our News page for dates and sign-up information each year.

Teen Programs

FCPL offers special programming for teens including:

  • Homework help and tutoring resources
  • Summer reading program for teens
  • Computer and internet access
  • Access to all digital resources with library card

Ask at any branch for current teen programming schedules.

Community Meeting Room

Meeting rooms are available for community use at some FCPL branches. Please call 304-465-0121 to check availability and reserve a room.
