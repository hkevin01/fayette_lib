Archives & Genealogy

Research your family history and explore Fayette County’s past.

Genealogy Research

The Fayette County Public Library maintains a genealogy collection to help you research your family history and local history. Our staff can assist with:

  • Fayette County birth, death, and marriage records
  • Local newspaper archives
  • Cemetery records
  • Historical photographs
  • Local history books and documents

The genealogy collection is primarily located at the Oak Hill Branch.

Online Genealogy Resources

Ancestry.com

Access Ancestry.com free from any FCPL branch computer. Search billions of historical records.

Visit Ancestry

FamilySearch — Free

Free genealogy records database maintained by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Over 3 billion records.
