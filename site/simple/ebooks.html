eBooks & Digital Media

Thousands of free eBooks, audiobooks, movies, and more — all free with your FCPL library card.

All digital resources are completely FREE with a valid FCPL library card. No late fees for digital checkouts!
Libby by OverDrive — Most Popular

Thousands of eBooks and audiobooks available on any device — phone, tablet, Kindle, or computer.

Borrow for 21 days with no late fees. Holds available for popular titles.

  • Works on Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android, Kindle
  • Borrow up to 10 titles at once
  • Download for offline reading
  • No late fees ever

Hoopla Digital — No Holds, Instant Access

Stream or download movies, TV shows, music, comic books, graphic novels, and audiobooks with no waiting and no holds. Borrow up to 10 titles per month.

  • No holds — borrow anything instantly
  • Movies, TV, music, comics, audiobooks, and eBooks
  • Up to 10 borrows per month at no cost
  • Works on any device with a web browser
