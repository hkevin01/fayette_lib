About Fayette County Public Libraries

Serving the communities of Fayette County, West Virginia since 1935.

Our Mission

The mission of Fayette County Public Libraries is to provide free access to information, education, culture, and recreation for all residents of Fayette County. We are committed to intellectual freedom, lifelong learning, and serving as a welcoming community space for everyone.

Free Services
  • Books, audiobooks, e-books and DVDs/videos for checkout
  • Magazines and newspapers
  • Computer access and Wi-Fi
  • Storytime hours
  • Summer reading programs
  • Notary service (must have photo ID)
  • Curriculum support
  • Tax forms (available in the Spring)
  • Scan documents or pictures to flash drive, email, or print
  • Voter registration assistance
  • Research and homework help
  • Genealogy research assistance
  • Children’s programs & storytimes
  • Adult programming and book clubs
  • Bookmobile home delivery for qualifying patrons
  • Homebound delivery service
  • Community meeting room use (by reservation)
Fee-Based Services
  • Copies and printing: $0.25 per page
  • Sending faxes: $3.00 first page, $1.00/page thereafter
  • Receiving faxes: $1.00 per page
  • Laminating service
  • Lost card replacement: $1.00
Library Card Eligibility

Any resident of Fayette County, WV is eligible for a free library card. Proof of county residency is required. Non-residents may obtain a card for an annual fee.

Board of Directors

The Fayette County Public Library Board of Trustees meets on the 2nd Tuesday of each quarter at 3:00 PM at the Administration Building, 531 Summit Street, Oak Hill.

For board meeting dates and board member information, please call 304-465-0121 or visit any branch.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I get a library card?
A: Visit any branch with proof of Fayette County residency. Cards are free for county residents.

Q: Can I return books to any branch?
A: Yes! You may return materials to any FCPL branch location.

Q: How do I renew my books?
A: Call your branch, visit in person, or renew online through the catalog at mlnapp.raleigh.lib.wv.us.

Q: Does the library have computers I can use?
A: Yes! Free internet computer access is available at all branches. Printing is $0.25 per page.

Q: How do I get free eBooks?
A: Download the Libby app (or visit libbyapp.com) and sign in with your FCPL library card number. See the eBooks page for more details.

Q: What if I can’t come to the library?
A: We offer a Homebound Delivery Program and a Bookmobile that visits many areas. Call 304-465-0121 to learn more.
