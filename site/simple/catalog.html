Catalog Search

Search the Fayette County Public Library catalog for books, DVDs, audiobooks, and more.

Search Our Catalog

Our online catalog lets you search for books, movies, music, and other materials available at any FCPL branch.

Open Library Catalog

You can search the catalog by:

  • Title of book, movie, or audiobook
  • Author name
  • Subject or topic
  • ISBN or barcode number
Statewide Catalog — WV Info Depot

Can’t find what you need at FCPL? Search the statewide catalog to find items at other West Virginia libraries. You can request items from other libraries through interlibrary loan.

Search WV Info Depot
