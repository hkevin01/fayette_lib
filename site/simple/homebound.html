Homebound Delivery Program

Free library service delivered directly to your door.

This program is completely free for qualifying Fayette County residents. No fees, no fines for homebound delivery materials.
About the Program

The FCPL Homebound Delivery Program provides free library services to Fayette County residents who are unable to visit a library branch or bookmobile stop due to physical disability, illness, or other circumstances that prevent them from leaving home.

Our dedicated program coordinator will work with you personally to select materials you’ll love — then deliver them right to your door.

Who Qualifies?

You may qualify for the Homebound program if you are:

  • Physically unable to visit a library or bookmobile stop
  • Homebound due to long-term illness or disability
  • Recovering from surgery or injury that prevents travel
  • A Fayette County resident with a valid (or new) library card
  • Unable to use transportation to reach a library branch
Materials We Deliver
  • Hardcover and paperback books
  • Large print books
  • Audiobooks on CD
  • Magazines and periodicals
  • DVDs and movies
  • Special interest materials
  • Materials from other WV libraries
  • Digital access setup assistance
How It Works

Step 1: Call us at 304-465-0121 to sign up.

Step 2: Our staff will ask about your reading preferences and interests.

Step 3: We select materials we think you will enjoy and arrange delivery.

Step 4: A library volunteer or staff member delivers the materials to your home.

Step 5: When ready, we pick up your materials and bring new ones!
